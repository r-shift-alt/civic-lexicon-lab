# Lexicon Entry Worksheet

## Purpose

This worksheet is the drafting template for version-one lexicon entries.

Use it to force consistency across entries and to prevent drift into unstructured essay writing.

## Evidence Quality Check

- Are the examples traceable?
- Do they show actual public or policy usage rather than only dictionary meaning?
- Does the mismatch claim rely on evidence rather than intuition alone?
- Is any uncertainty labeled clearly?

## Recommendation Check

- Could a user act on this entry immediately?
- Is there at least one clear warning?
- Is there at least one positive recommendation rather than only caution?
- Would this still be understandable without verbal explanation from the project team?

## Completion Rule

An entry counts as complete only if:
- all major blocks are filled;
- at least 2 real examples are included;
- the guidance is specific enough to change a practical wording decision;
- confidence and uncertainty are stated explicitly.
