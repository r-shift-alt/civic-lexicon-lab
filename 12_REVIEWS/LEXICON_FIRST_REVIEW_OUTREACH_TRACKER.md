## Slot Selection Logic

The first pass should still stay narrow.
The three reviewer slots below are chosen to cover the strongest user-job fits already implied by the packet:
1. one editor-facing slot for register and wording decisions;
2. one translation-facing slot for false-equivalence and gloss decisions;
3. one policy/legal-language slot for institutional and citizenship-language judgment.
