# Lexicon Review Synthesis Memo Template

## Purpose

Use this template after the first proxy-user review cycle for the pilot packet in `12_REVIEWS/LEXICON_PILOT_REVIEWER_PACKET.md`.

It is meant to compress a small number of reviewer responses into one short decision-ready memo.
Keep it brief, comparative, and tied to actual wording or format decisions.

## Use Rule

- Target review count: 2 to 3 reviewers.
- Fill this out only after at least 2 packet responses exist.
- Quote or paraphrase only what is needed to support a decision.
- Keep the memo short enough that a maintainer can decide whether to continue, revise, or stop without re-reading all raw notes.
